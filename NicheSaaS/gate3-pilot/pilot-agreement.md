# Design Partner Pilot — Term Sheet

> **This is a term sheet, not a contract.** Plain language, written to be
> readable in the room and to make the terms explicit before anyone signs
> anything. The liability and data sections carry the most weight and are the
> ones most likely to need changing for your jurisdiction and insurance
> position.

---

**Between:** [Your company] ("we") and [Customer] ("you")
**Starting:** [date] · **Term:** 3 months · **Fee:** $[N] per month

## What we do

1. You send us inspection audio and photographs. We return draft inspection
   reports, with standards citations, and a priced repair proposal from your
   price book.
2. Drafts come back within one business day of receiving complete files.
3. Up to 40 inspections per month. Above that we'll agree a number before
   continuing.
4. We configure your report templates, your jurisdictions' output formats, and
   your price book at no charge during the pilot.
5. You get a direct line to us — phone and email, same-day response on weekdays.

## What you do

1. Name one person as our contact.
2. Send inspection files within 48 hours of the inspection.
3. Give us about 30 minutes a week of honest feedback, including when the output
   is wrong.
4. Pay monthly, in advance.

## Review and sign-off — the important part

**Every report we produce is a draft.** A qualified person on your side reviews
and signs it before it goes to any Authority Having Jurisdiction, insurer, or
building owner.

We do not file anything. We do not attest to anything. Professional
responsibility for every filing stays exactly where it sits today — with your
licensed personnel.

You agree not to submit any report we produce without that review.

## Accuracy

We are building this and it is not finished. Reports will contain errors,
including missed deficiencies and, occasionally, findings that should not be
there. That is why review is required and why we would rather show you the
errors than hide them.

We make no warranty that output is complete, accurate, or fit for filing.

## Liability

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you have paid
us. Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss.

Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## Your data

1. Your inspection files, reports, price book, and templates remain yours.
2. We use them to produce your reports and to evaluate and improve the system.
3. We will not share them with anyone else, or use them to produce output for
   another customer.
4. On request, at any time, we will delete them and confirm in writing when it's
   done.
5. Reports we have already delivered are yours to keep, in your format,
   permanently — including after this agreement ends.

## Standards material

Our reports are produced from our own inspection procedure library. That library
describes what to check in our own words and cites the clause of the published
standard each procedure relates to. It is not the standard, does not reproduce
the standard's text, and is not a substitute for it.

You hold your own licensed copy of the applicable standards, and your reviewer
relies on that copy — not on us — for authoritative wording.

Neither of us sends the other copyrighted standards text.

## Confidentiality

Each of us keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it
only for this pilot. Survives the agreement by two years.

## Feedback

Suggestions you give us about the product are ours to use, freely, with no
obligation. This doesn't affect your ownership of your data.

## Ending it

Either of us can end this with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for
the current month are not refunded; nothing further is owed.

At the end, if you want to continue, we'll agree annual terms separately. There
is no automatic renewal and no obligation either way.

## Not exclusive

Nothing stops you using any other product, or us working with anyone else.

### Notes for you, not for the customer

- **The review clause is the load-bearing one.** It's what keeps you out of the
  professional-liability chain, so it is the clause to check against whatever
  E&O cover you end up carrying — the two have to line up.
- **The standards clause is now a positive claim, not a disclaimer.** It states
  that the library is ours. Make sure that is actually true before signing it —
  the wording here is only as good as the authoring discipline in
  [`../gate2-harness/fixtures/library/README.md`](../gate2-harness/fixtures/library/README.md).
- **Deliberately easy to leave.** Thirty days, no lock-in, keep your reports.
  That is the honest offer at this stage and it removes the objection that
  otherwise kills the deal. The switching cost you eventually want comes from
  accumulated history, not from contract terms.
