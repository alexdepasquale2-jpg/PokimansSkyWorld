## SYN-25 — Water-based suppression: inspection, testing, maintenance

### SYN-25-05 Sprinklers

**SYN-25-05.1.1** Sprinklers shall be inspected from floor level annually.
Sprinklers showing signs of corrosion, physical damage, paint or coating not
applied by the manufacturer, or loading shall be replaced.

**SYN-25-05.1.2** A minimum clearance of 18 inches (450 mm) shall be maintained
below the deflector of each sprinkler. Storage, ductwork, or fixtures encroaching
on this clearance shall be recorded as a deficiency.

**SYN-25-05.2.1** Spare sprinklers shall be maintained on the premises in a
cabinet, in a quantity appropriate to the number of installed sprinklers, together
with a wrench of the type required for each sprinkler present.

**SYN-25-05.3.4** Sprinklers installed in areas subject to corrosive atmospheres
shall be inspected annually and replaced where corrosion is observed on the
frame, deflector, or operating element.

### SYN-25-08 Gauges

**SYN-25-08.2.1** Gauges shall be inspected monthly to verify they are in good
condition and that normal pressure is being maintained.

**SYN-25-08.2.2** Gauges shall be replaced or recalibrated at intervals not
exceeding five years. A gauge whose calibration date cannot be established shall
be treated as exceeding the interval.

**SYN-25-08.2.3** A gauge with a fogged, cracked, or illegible face shall be
replaced. A gauge that cannot be read cannot demonstrate compliance.

### SYN-25-11 Water supply and flow testing

**SYN-25-11.3.1** A main drain test shall be conducted annually at each system
riser to verify the water supply has not been compromised.

**SYN-25-11.3.2** Where a main drain test produces a residual pressure that
differs by more than 10 percent from the previous test on record, the cause shall
be determined and corrected.

**SYN-25-11.4.2** Results of each main drain test shall be recorded, including
static pressure, residual pressure, and the date of the previous test used for
comparison.

### SYN-25-13 Valves

**SYN-25-13.2.5** Control valves shall be inspected quarterly to verify they are
in the normal open position, sealed or supervised, accessible, free of external
leakage, and provided with an identification sign.

**SYN-25-13.2.6** Each control valve shall be provided with a permanently marked,
weatherproof identification sign indicating the portion of the system it
controls. A missing or illegible sign shall be recorded as a deficiency.

**SYN-25-13.3.1** Control valves shall be operated through their full range and
returned to normal position annually.

**SYN-25-13.6.1** Backflow prevention assemblies shall be tested annually by a
qualified individual, and the test results shall be recorded.

### SYN-25-14 Fire department connections

**SYN-25-14.1.1** Fire department connections shall be inspected quarterly to
verify they are visible, accessible, and unobstructed, that couplings and swivels
rotate freely, that caps or plugs are in place, and that the identification sign
is present and legible.

**SYN-25-14.1.3** An obstructed fire department connection shall be recorded as a
critical deficiency and corrected without delay.

## SYN-72 — Signalling systems: inspection and testing

### SYN-72-14 Inspection and testing

**SYN-72-14.3.1** Control equipment shall be inspected annually to verify that
trouble signals, supervisory signals, and alarm indicators function, and that no
condition is standing at the panel.

**SYN-72-14.3.2** A trouble or supervisory condition present at the panel at the
time of inspection shall be recorded, together with the cause where it can be
determined.

**SYN-72-14.4.2** Secondary power supplies shall be tested annually. Batteries
showing corrosion, swelling, or leakage shall be replaced.

**SYN-72-14.4.5** Batteries shall be marked with the date of installation and
replaced within the interval recommended by the manufacturer.

**SYN-72-14.5.1** Notification appliances shall be tested to verify audibility
and, where required, visibility throughout the protected area.

**SYN-72-14.5.3** A notification appliance that is obstructed, painted over, or
physically damaged shall be recorded as a deficiency and restored to service.

### SYN-72-17 Initiating devices

**SYN-72-17.2.1** Smoke detectors shall be tested annually for sensitivity within
the range marked by the manufacturer.

**SYN-72-17.2.4** Detectors shall be free of paint, dust accumulation, and
physical obstruction. A detector with an obstructed inlet shall be cleaned or
replaced.

**SYN-72-17.6.2** Waterflow switches shall be tested semiannually, and the signal
shall be verified at the control unit and at the supervising station where one is
provided.

## Severity guidance

**Critical** — the condition prevents the system from performing its function on
demand: an obstructed fire department connection, a closed control valve, a
panel in alarm or trouble with an undetermined cause.

**Major** — the condition materially degrades performance or cannot be shown to
comply: a gauge past its calibration interval, an untested backflow assembly, a
main drain residual outside the 10 percent band.

**Minor** — the condition is a documentation or accessibility defect that does
not itself impair function: a missing valve identification sign, spare sprinklers
absent from the cabinet.

**Observation** — worth recording for the owner but not a deficiency against this
standard.
