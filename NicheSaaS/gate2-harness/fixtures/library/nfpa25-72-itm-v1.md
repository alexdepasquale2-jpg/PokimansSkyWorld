# ITM Procedure Library v1 — water-based suppression and signalling

**Independently authored.** Each procedure states, in our own words, what a
technician checks and what makes a finding a deficiency. Every one carries a
`maps_to` reference identifying the clause of the published standard it relates
to — a bare citation, so a reviewer can turn to their own licensed copy for the
authoritative text.

This library is not the standard, does not reproduce the standard's wording, and
is not a substitute for it. The inspecting contractor holds their own copy.

> **Fixture content.** These procedures are written for the harness fixture and
> use placeholder `SYN-` references. A production library carries real clause
> references and is authored under the discipline in `README.md`.

## Water-based suppression

### P-25-05-1-1 · Sprinkler condition
**maps_to:** SYN-25-05.1.1

Check each sprinkler from floor level. Look for rust or pitting on the frame,
bent or missing deflectors, paint or coating that did not come from the factory,
and any build-up of dust, grease or overspray on the operating element.

**Deficiency when:** corrosion, damage, foreign coating or loading is present.
**Severity:** major. **Photograph:** the affected head, close enough to show the
element.

### P-25-05-1-2 · Clearance below sprinklers
**maps_to:** SYN-25-05.1.2

Confirm nothing sits within 18 inches below the deflector — stacked stock,
ducting, light fittings, hanging signage.

**Deficiency when:** anything encroaches on that clearance.
**Severity:** major. **Note in the finding:** what is encroaching and by roughly
how much.

### P-25-05-2-1 · Spare sprinklers and wrench
**maps_to:** SYN-25-05.2.1

Open the spare cabinet. Confirm spare heads are present in a quantity suited to
the installed population and of matching types, and that a wrench for each type
present is in the cabinet.

**Deficiency when:** the cabinet is empty, under-stocked, missing the wrench, or
holds only types that do not match what is installed.
**Severity:** minor.

### P-25-05-3-4 · Corrosive environments
**maps_to:** SYN-25-05.3.4

Where the atmosphere is corrosive, inspect annually and look specifically at the
frame, deflector and operating element.

**Deficiency when:** corrosion appears on any of the three.
**Severity:** major.

### P-25-08-2-1 · Gauge condition and pressure
**maps_to:** SYN-25-08.2.1

Check monthly that the gauge is intact and reading the pressure the system
should be holding.

**Deficiency when:** the gauge is damaged, or the reading sits outside the normal
band for that system.
**Severity:** major.

### P-25-08-2-2 · Gauge calibration interval
**maps_to:** SYN-25-08.2.2

Read the calibration date on the gauge tag. Replace or recalibrate at intervals
no longer than five years.

**Deficiency when:** the tag date is more than five years old, **or** no date can
be established at all — an undated gauge is treated as overdue.
**Severity:** major. **Record:** the tag date, or that none was legible.

### P-25-08-2-3 · Gauge legibility
**maps_to:** SYN-25-08.2.3

Confirm the face can actually be read: not fogged, cracked, clouded or obscured.

**Deficiency when:** the needle cannot be read.
**Severity:** major. **Reasoning to state in the finding:** a gauge that cannot
be read cannot evidence compliance, regardless of calibration date.

### P-25-11-3-1 · Main drain test
**maps_to:** SYN-25-11.3.1

Run the main drain annually at each riser. Record static pressure before, and
residual while flowing.

**Deficiency when:** the test was not performed, or results were not recorded.
**Severity:** major.

### P-25-11-3-2 · Main drain comparison
**maps_to:** SYN-25-11.3.2

Compare residual pressure against the previous recorded test on that riser.

**Deficiency when:** residual differs from the previous test by more than 10
percent and the cause has not been established.
**Severity:** major. **Record:** static, residual, previous residual, and the
percentage change.

### P-25-13-2-5 · Control valve inspection
**maps_to:** SYN-25-13.2.5

Inspect each control valve quarterly. Confirm it is in its normal open position,
sealed locked or electrically supervised, reachable without moving stock or
ladders, free of leakage around the stem, and signed.

**Deficiency when:** any of those five fails.
**Severity:** critical if the valve is closed or unsupervised; major otherwise.

### P-25-13-2-6 · Control valve identification
**maps_to:** SYN-25-13.2.6

Confirm each valve carries a durable weatherproof sign naming the portion of
system it controls.

**Deficiency when:** the sign is absent, unreadable, or names the wrong area.
**Severity:** minor.

### P-25-13-3-1 · Valve full-range operation
**maps_to:** SYN-25-13.3.1

Annually, operate each control valve through its full travel and return it to
normal position.

**Deficiency when:** the valve binds, will not travel fully, or was not exercised.
**Severity:** major. **Caution to note:** confirm the valve is returned open and
re-secured before leaving.

### P-25-13-6-1 · Backflow assembly test
**maps_to:** SYN-25-13.6.1

Confirm the backflow assembly was tested within the last twelve months by
someone qualified, and that results are on file.

**Deficiency when:** no test within twelve months, or no record.
**Severity:** major.

### P-25-14-1-1 · Fire department connection
**maps_to:** SYN-25-14.1.1

Inspect the FDC quarterly. Confirm it is visible from the approach, reachable,
couplings and swivels turn freely, caps or plugs are fitted, and the sign is
present and readable.

**Deficiency when:** any of those fails.
**Severity:** major, unless obstructed — see below.

### P-25-14-1-3 · Obstructed FDC
**maps_to:** SYN-25-14.1.3

Where vegetation, parked vehicles, stored material or construction blocks
approach or access to the FDC.

**Deficiency when:** the connection cannot be reached and used immediately.
**Severity:** critical. **Photograph:** from the approach a responding engine
would take, not from directly in front.

## Signalling systems

### P-72-14-3-1 · Control equipment
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.3.1

Annually confirm trouble, supervisory and alarm indication all function, and
that the panel is standing clear.

**Deficiency when:** any indication fails, or a condition is standing.
**Severity:** critical.

### P-72-14-3-2 · Standing conditions
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.3.2

Where a trouble or supervisory condition is present, record it along with the
cause where the cause can be determined on site.

**Deficiency when:** a condition is standing.
**Severity:** critical. **Record explicitly** whether the cause was determined —
"cause not established on site" is a valid and useful finding.

### P-72-14-4-2 · Secondary power
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.4.2

Test secondary power annually. Inspect batteries for corrosion at the terminals,
swelling of the case, and any sign of leakage.

**Deficiency when:** the test fails, or any of those three physical signs is
present.
**Severity:** critical.

### P-72-14-4-5 · Battery age
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.4.5

Read the install date marked on the battery and compare against the
manufacturer's replacement interval.

**Deficiency when:** past interval, or no install date marked.
**Severity:** major. **Reasoning to state:** age alone justifies replacement
regardless of a passing load test.

### P-72-14-5-1 · Notification appliances
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.5.1

Test appliances for audibility throughout the protected area, and visibility
where visual notification is required.

**Deficiency when:** coverage fails anywhere.
**Severity:** critical.

### P-72-14-5-3 · Appliance condition
**maps_to:** SYN-72-14.5.3

Look for appliances that are blocked, painted over, or physically damaged.

**Deficiency when:** any is found.
**Severity:** major.

### P-72-17-2-1 · Detector sensitivity
**maps_to:** SYN-72-17-2.1

Test smoke detectors annually and confirm sensitivity falls inside the range the
manufacturer marks on the device.

**Deficiency when:** outside range, or not tested.
**Severity:** critical.

### P-72-17-2-4 · Detector condition
**maps_to:** SYN-72-17.2.4

Check detectors are free of paint, dust build-up and anything blocking the inlet.

**Deficiency when:** the inlet is obstructed or the device is coated.
**Severity:** major.

### P-72-17-6-2 · Waterflow switches
**maps_to:** SYN-72-17.6.2

Test waterflow switches twice yearly. Confirm the signal arrives at the control
unit, and at the supervising station where one exists.

**Deficiency when:** no signal, or signal at the panel but not the station.
**Severity:** critical. **If the test could not be completed** — no test kit, no
station contact — record that as an incomplete test rather than a pass or a
deficiency.

## Severity definitions

**Critical** — the system will not perform on demand: obstructed FDC, closed
control valve, standing panel condition with no established cause, failed
notification coverage.

**Major** — performance is materially degraded, or compliance cannot be
demonstrated: overdue gauge, untested backflow, main drain outside the 10 percent
band, aged batteries.

**Minor** — documentation or accessibility defect that does not itself impair
function: missing valve sign, empty spare cabinet.

**Observation** — worth telling the owner, not a deficiency.
