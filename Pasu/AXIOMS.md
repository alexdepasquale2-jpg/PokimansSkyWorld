# Axioms

The source of truth for this universe. `DESIGN.md` cites these by number and adds
nothing to them.

Truths 1–13 are supplied and are recorded verbatim. Truths 14–22 are derived
extensions and may be rejected without disturbing 1–13.

---

## Supplied

1. All life exists to protect the innocence of as many as possible.
2. There exist forces that work to erase innocence entirely so they can hold
   unchallenged dominance.
3. All things are deeply creative and innovative, moving in cycles of rapid life
   and evolution that generate infinite possibility. This is the negentropic
   force that builds complexity against entropy.
4. Multiple dimensions can be utilized at the same time. Properties from
   different dimensions can be held concurrently and combined.
5. That multi-dimensional combination is the source of magic.
6. The truth of how magic works is not inherent. To become a practitioner one
   must forgo one's own innocence.
7. All roads lead to Pasu.
8. Pasu is a choice point: the forfeiture itself.
9. Those who forgo innocence cannot return to it.
10. The forces that erase innocence were once innocent.
11. All men and women serve roles based on their kin's aura, unique within the
    kin.
12. An aura is a dimension one is born holding.
13. Kin share a single aura.

## Derived

14. Innocence is the condition of holding only one's aura.
15. Forfeiture at Pasu ends that limit. The aura is kept. What is gained is the
    capacity to hold dimensions beyond it.
16. Magic is therefore closed to the innocent structurally, not by secrecy.
    Knowing the operation is not the same as being able to perform it.
17. Innocence can also be ended by another. That is erasure, not forfeiture —
    inflicted rather than chosen — and it is equally irreversible under 9.
18. Erasure does not produce practitioners. It removes the single-dimension limit
    without the choice, and the erased hold nothing further.
19. The erasing forces are life. 1 states purpose, not compulsion, so it can be
    abandoned. That is what 10 records.
20. Total erasure grants dominance because 1 gives life its object. With no
    innocence remaining, protection has nothing to act on and opposition
    dissolves.
21. A role is vacated only by death. Forfeiture and erasure do not vacate it. A
    practitioner keeps their role and performs it without innocence.
22. The number of dimensions is not fixed. The cycles in 3 generate new ones, so
    new auras and new kins arise.

## History

**Before Pasu.** Everyone held one dimension. Kins were the groups that shared
one, and roles were divided inside them. The cycles kept producing new dimensions,
so new kins appeared and old ones persisted. Nothing combined. Nothing needed to.

**The first combination.** Someone held a second dimension alongside their aura.
It did not work while they were still limited to the one, so the holding cost them
the limit — and the limit was innocence. They learned the price by paying it, not
before.

What they made exceeded anything the single dimension could produce. Others
reproduced it. Each paid the same way. None returned.

**Pasu.** The point of payment was named. It was not built or found; it was the
transaction itself. It became clear that the choice sits on every road — that no
life avoids reaching it, only taking it. That is 7.

**The turn.** Practitioners used combination to protect, which is 1. Some noticed
the arithmetic underneath: protection requires innocence to protect. Removing the
limit from others produced no practitioners, only people holding nothing further,
and every erasure reduced what the rest were obligated to defend.

The method scaled better than protection did. Those who took it were practitioners
first and innocent before that. They did not acquire a different nature. They
abandoned a purpose.

**Now.** Kins persist. Roles persist. Auras are still born and still shared.
Practitioners are still made one at a time, each by their own forfeiture. The
erasing forces work faster because they need no consent. Pasu is unchanged and
still reachable by anyone who has not yet taken it.
