how it works

the run

An erasing force crosses the region. It strips innocence from settlements. The erased stay alive; they stop counting and cannot be restored. You cannot save everything. Choose.

three actions a turn

Move by tapping an adjacent node. Shelter a settlement to make it cost the force an extra turn. Search a site to locate a dimension. Shape to make something out of what you hold.

dimensions

Every dimension is a set of properties with magnitudes. Held dimensions sum. The sum decides what happens — there are no recipes.

Pasu

Innocence is holding only your aura. You may find other dimensions but you cannot hold them. Pasu ends that limit and cannot be undone. Take it too early and you have little to combine. Take it too late and the erasure has run.

Each dimension held past the first strains you. A strained holding does not fail — it resolves wrong.

death

The force wounds what it reaches. If it reaches you while you are innocent, it erases you: you gain nothing and can never take Pasu this run. At three wounds you die, your role vacates, and another of your kin takes it — sharing your aura, holding a different role, innocent again.

between runs

Nothing carries but knowledge. No stats, no gear, no unlocks. The codex remembers what you saw a sum do.
